TimeSnap Apps Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 September 2026

TimeSnap does not have servers. We do not collect, receive, transmit to ourselves, or sell any of your personal information. Everything you record stays on your device, unless you personally choose to back it up to your own Google Drive.

This policy explains what TimeSnap ("the app") stores, where it goes, and how to remove it.

1. About the developer

TimeSnap is developed and published by MN Momently Studio. For any privacy question or request, contact mnmomentlystudio@outlook.com.

2. What the app stores on your device

All of the following is written to a private database inside the app's own storage area on your phone. It is not readable by other apps, and it is never sent to us.

DataPurpose
Activity names and colours you createTo label and display what you are tracking.
Time entries: start time, end time, duration, and any note you typeThe core record of how you spent your time.
Goals: target minutes, period, and which activity they apply toTo show progress against the goals you set.
App settings: day start time, week start day, notification and sync preferencesTo keep the app behaving the way you configured it.
Your Pro entitlement statusTo unlock paid features without re-checking Google Play on every launch.

Notes on time entries are free text. Please avoid typing information you would not want stored on your device or copied into your Google Drive backups.

3. Google account and Google Drive

Connecting Google Drive is optional. Your data is stored locally on your device unless you connect your Google Drive account and a backup runs.

If you do connect Drive:

  • Your Google account email address and display name are stored on your device so the app can show you which account is connected. They are not transmitted to us.
  • An OAuth access token issued by Google is stored on your device so the app can upload backups. It is not transmitted to us.
  • The app requests only the drive.file and drive.appdata permissions. These are deliberately narrow: they let TimeSnap see and manage only the files it creates itself. TimeSnap cannot read, list, or access any other document, photo, or file in your Google Drive.
  • Backups are written as CSV files into a TimeSnap folder in your own Google Drive. They contain your activity names, time entries, notes, and goals. This is your storage, under your Google account — we have no access to it at any point.

Once data is in your Google Drive it is also governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Purchases

TimeSnap Pro is a one-time in-app purchase processed entirely by Google Play Billing. The app receives only a confirmation that a purchase exists and is valid. It never sees, handles, or stores your name, card number, billing address, or any other payment detail. Refunds and billing queries are handled by Google Play.

5. Permissions the app requests

PermissionPurpose
INTERNETOnly to upload backups to your Google Drive and to talk to Google Play Billing.
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATETo avoid attempting a sync when you are offline.
POST_NOTIFICATIONSTo send the tracking reminders you enable in settings. You can decline or revoke this at any time.

TimeSnap does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, calendar, call logs, SMS, or the files on your device.

6. What we do not do

  • We do not operate any server that receives your data.
  • We do not use analytics, telemetry, or crash-reporting services.
  • We do not include advertising networks or advertising identifiers.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites.
  • We do not build profiles, and we do not make automated decisions about you.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with anyone.

7. Retention and how to delete your data

Because we never receive your data, we cannot hold it nor delete on your behalf. You remain in complete control:

  • Delete individual records — remove any time entry, activity, or goal from within the app at any time.
  • Delete everything on the device — uninstalling TimeSnap permanently removes its database, settings, stored Google account details, and OAuth token from your phone. You can also use Android Settings → Apps → TimeSnap → Storage → Clear data.
  • Delete your backups — open Google Drive and delete the TimeSnap folder and its CSV files, then empty your Drive trash.
  • Revoke Drive access — visit myaccount.google.com/permissions, select TimeSnap, and remove its access. This immediately invalidates the stored token.

If you need help with any of these steps, contact mnmomentlystudio@outlook.com and we will walk you through them.

8. Children

TimeSnap is a general-audience productivity tool. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone — including children — because we do not collect information at all.

9. Security

Your records are held in the app's private storage area, which Android isolates from other applications. Data in transit to Google Drive is sent over encrypted HTTPS connections. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, but because your information never reaches a server of ours, there is no central database of TimeSnap users that could be breached.

10. Your rights

Privacy laws including the Australian Privacy Principles, the GDPR, and the CCPA give you rights to access, correct, export, and delete your personal information. In TimeSnap you can exercise all of these directly: your data is on your device, you can view and edit it in the app, save a complete copy of it using Local Backup & Restore, and delete it as described in section 7. Local Backup is available on both the free and Pro plans, so exporting your data never requires a purchase. (CSV export is a separate Pro convenience feature.) We hold no copy, so there is no request to make of us.

11. Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this page will be updated and the date at the top revised. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes on Google Play.

12. Contact

Questions about this policy or about privacy in TimeSnap: mnmomentlystudio@outlook.com
