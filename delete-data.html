How to Delete Your TimeSnap Data

TimeSnap, by MN Momently Studio · Last updated 5 September 2026

TimeSnap has no servers and no user accounts. We never receive your data, so there is no request to submit and no waiting period — you can delete everything yourself, immediately, using the steps below.

Steps to delete your data

  1. Delete individual records. Open TimeSnap and remove any time entry, activity, or goal directly in the app.
  2. Delete everything stored on your device. Either uninstall TimeSnap, or go to Android Settings → Apps → TimeSnap → Storage → Clear data. Both permanently erase the app's entire database, your settings, the connected Google account details, and the stored authorisation token.
  3. Delete your Google Drive backups. Open Google Drive, find the TimeSnap folder, and delete it. Then open Drive's Trash and choose Empty trash to remove the files permanently.
  4. Revoke TimeSnap's access to your Google account. Go to myaccount.google.com/permissions, select TimeSnap, and choose Remove access. This immediately invalidates the stored token.
  5. Need help? Email mnmomentlystudio@outlook.com and we will walk you through any of the steps above. Because we hold no copy of your data, we cannot perform the deletion on your behalf — but we will help you complete it.

What is kept, and for how long

MN Momently Studio retains nothing. We operate no servers and hold no copy of your information at any point, so there is no retention period on our side — it is zero.

Two things outside our control are worth knowing about:

  • Google Drive trash — up to 30 days. Files you delete from Drive sit in your Trash and are removed automatically after about 30 days. Emptying the Trash yourself (step 3) deletes them immediately.
  • Google Play purchase records. If you bought TimeSnap Pro, Google Play keeps a record of that transaction under its own retention policy, for refunds, billing, and tax purposes. That record belongs to Google, not to us, and it contains no time-tracking data. See Google's Privacy Policy. Because the purchase is tied to your Google account, reinstalling TimeSnap and choosing "Restore purchases" will restore Pro — no time-tracking data is restored with it.

Deleting your data does not cancel or refund a purchase. Refunds are handled by Google Play.
